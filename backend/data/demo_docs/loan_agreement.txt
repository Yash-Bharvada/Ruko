PERSONAL LOAN AND SECURITY AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement is entered into on 15th March 2026 between Apex Micro Finance Limited (the "Lender") and Ramesh Kumar Sharma (the "Borrower").

1. PRINCIPAL LOAN AMOUNT & DISBURSEMENT
The Lender agrees to disburse a personal loan of INR 2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only) to the Borrower's designated bank account within 3 business days of signing.

2. INTEREST RATE & EQUATED MONTHLY INSTALLMENT (EMI)
The loan carries a fixed interest rate of 14.5% per annum. The Borrower shall repay the loan in 24 equal monthly installments of INR 12,060 due on the 5th of each calendar month.

3. PROCESSING FEE & ADMINISTRATIVE CHARGES
A non-refundable one-time upfront processing fee of INR 3,500 (plus applicable GST) shall be deducted directly from the disbursed principal loan amount.

4. LATE PAYMENT PENALTY & DEFAULT
In the event of delay in EMI payment beyond the due date, a penal interest of 2% per month will be charged on the outstanding overdue amount until full settlement.

5. PRECLOSURE AND FORECLOSURE CHARGES
The Borrower has the right to prepay the remaining loan balance after paying a minimum of 6 EMIs, subject to a foreclosure fee of 3% on the outstanding principal balance.

6. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts in Mumbai.
